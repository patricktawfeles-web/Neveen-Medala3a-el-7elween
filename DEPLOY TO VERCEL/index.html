Aftercare & questions · العناية والأسئلة

Keep them flawless

Hard gel & soft gel aftercare, plus the answers clients ask most. كل اللي محتاجين تعرفيه عن العناية بعد الحجز

💎 Hard Gel Aftercare

  • First 2 hours: keep nails dry — no washing dishes or long showers. أول ساعتين خليهم ناشفين تماماً.
  • Never use them as tools: no opening tins, peeling stickers or scratching labels.
  • Cuticle oil daily: twice a day keeps the seal strong and prevents lifting. زيت الكيوتيكل يومياً مرتين.
  • Gloves for cleaning: chemicals are the fastest way to dull your shine.
  • Refill every 3–4 weeks — book early, growth lines are easier to fix than breaks.

🌸 Soft Gel & Gelish Aftercare

  • Wait 1 hour before water, sauna or heavy workout after your appointment.
  • Wear gloves for housework — gel stays shiny when it isn't attacked by chemicals.
  • Push, don't pick: if a corner lifts, book a repair (150 EGP/nail) instead of peeling it off.
  • Moisturise daily: hand cream + cuticle oil keeps the manicure looking fresh. ترطيب يومي للأيدين.
  • Removal only in studio: never file or soak it off yourself — that's how nails get thin.

3 to 4 weeks with proper aftercare. We recommend a refill at week 3–4 to keep the apex balanced and prevent lifting. من 3 لـ 4 أسابيع لو ملتزمتش بالعناية، والريفيل بعد كده بيحافظ على الشكل.

Hard gel builds length and strength — it's a sculpted extension that can't be soaked off. Soft gel (tips + gelish) is lighter, more flexible and soak-off friendly, perfect for natural-looking length. الهارد جل للطول والقوة والسكالبت، والسوفت جل أخف وبيتشال بالمحلول ومناسب للطبيعة.
